Get involved

Volunteer with Punjabiforce.

However you contribute, you carry one title: Punjabiforce Community Ambassador. Alongside it, Ambassadors take on a focus area that suits their strengths and the time they have.

Focus areas

Where Ambassadors lend a hand.

Pick the area closest to your strengths. These aren't separate job titles — they're the shape your Ambassador role takes, and you're welcome to move between them as the community grows.

How it works

Four steps, no pressure.

01

Register interest

Fill in the join form and tick Volunteer. A name, an email and your LinkedIn is plenty.

02

Informal chat

One of the founders gets in touch. No interview, no CV, no test.

03

Find your fit

We talk through what you enjoy and how much time you actually have.

04

Join the group

You're introduced to the wider Ambassador group and get stuck in.

Register interest

Tell us where you'd like to help.

No experience bar, no minimum commitment — just an interest in helping the community grow.

What we'll ask you

  • Name and email, so we can reach you
  • LinkedIn profile, so we can introduce you to the team
  • Focus area you're drawn to, or "not sure yet"
  • Anything else you'd like us to know

Takes about two minutes. Volunteer is ticked for you, and one of the founders will be in touch for an informal chat.
